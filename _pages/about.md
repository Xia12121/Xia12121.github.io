Currently, I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Industrial Engineering Systems at the University of Oklahoma. During my undergraduate studies, I was an RA of Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University supervised by Prof. [Ricky Yuen-tan Hou](https://staff.uic.edu.cn/rickyhou/en). 

If you are interested in my work, feel free to reach out!
